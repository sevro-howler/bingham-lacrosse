#### Station A: Offensive Sets — Live Ball

**Objective:** Run Pineapple, Peanut Butter, and Lion with a live ball. Full speed passing, moving feet, no standing around.
**Live players:** 4-5 offense (add 2-3 defense after first 5 min)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. Cones marking positions.

**Drill (15 min per group):**
- **First 5 min — Offense only, live ball:**
  - Coach calls "PINEAPPLE!", "PEANUT BUTTER!", or "[Name] is LION!"
  - Players set up and run the play at FULL SPEED
  - Ball must move — pass and relocate, pass and relocate
  - No standing with the ball. Catch → pass or dodge → shoot.
  - Reset, next call. Rotate positions every 2 reps.

- **Next 5 min — Add 2 defenders:**
  - Same calls, but now defense is live
  - Offense must pass around the defense to get open looks
  - Defense plays positioning — no lunging, guide them

- **Final 5 min — 3v2 with called plays:**
  - 3 offense vs 2 defense, coach calls the set
  - Offense runs the play, must complete 2 passes before shooting
  - Defense plays Ball-Help, switches on every pass
  - Goal or turnover, next group

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Feet moving!"** — No standing still with or without the ball
- **"Pass and move!"** — Don't pass and watch
- **"Run the play!"** — Trust the system

**Key words:** "Pineapple!", "Peanut Butter!", "[Name] is Lion!", "Pass and move!"

---

#### Station B: Defense — "Slow and Guide"

**Objective:** Defensive positioning. Slow the attacker down, guide him to the sideline. Don't overcommit, don't lunge, don't blow past. Patient defense.
**Live players:** 2 at a time (1v1)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. 2 lines on opposite sides, ~15 yards from goal. Mirrored on both sides.

**Drill (15 min per group):**
- Attacker carries ball toward goal
- Defender gets in front — body between attacker and goal
- **SLOW HIM DOWN** — match his speed, don't rush in
- **GUIDE HIM OUT** — push toward sideline, not middle
- Don't overcommit: if you lunge, he's past you

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Slow down!"** — Match his speed, don't sprint at him
- **"Guide him out!"** — Push to sideline
- **"Stay in front!"** — Don't get beside him
- **"Patient!"** — Don't lunge, don't reach, wait for help
- **"Feet first!"** — Slide, stay low, stay balanced

**Progression:**
- **First 5 min:** Half speed — defender practices slowing and matching
- **Next 5 min:** 3/4 speed — attacker tries harder, defender stays patient
- **Final 5 min:** Full speed 1v1 — live, attacker tries to score

**Key words:** "Slow down!", "Guide him out!", "Patient!", "Don't overcommit!"

### 7:00-7:30 — 4v4 Live (30 min)

**4v4 half field. Full set of 4 rotates on goal or turnover.**

**Same rules as last week:**
1. **Must complete 3 passes before shooting** — no hero ball
2. **Can't pass back to the passer** — ball moves around
3. **Coach calls the play** — run it or lose possession
4. **Hero ball = whistle = turnover**

**Focus:**
- **Offense: "PASS AND MOVE! RUN THE PLAY!"** — feet moving, ball moving, no standing
- **Defense: "SLOW AND GUIDE! DON'T OVERCOMMIT!"** — patient, position, push out

**Coach calls out:**
- Standing still with ball: "PASS AND MOVE!"
- Off-ball standing: "GET OPEN!"
- Defender lunging: "PATIENT! Don't overcommit!"
- Defender blowing past: "SLOW DOWN! Guide him!"
- Good passing: "THAT'S IT! Ball's moving!"
- Good defense: "THAT'S IT! Patient! Guide him out!"

Goal or turnover, new 8, go.

## Key Terms for Tonight

| **Wall Ball** | Reps on the wall, both hands, quick stick |
