#### Station A: CLEAR — "Tightrope & Middle"

**Objective:** Goalie saves, defenders pop out to outlets, one middie runs the tightrope (along the sideline), one middie runs to the middle of the field. Long passes to move the ball out.
**Live players:** 6 (goalie + 2 defense + 2 middies + 1 attack at midfield)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. Cones marking:
- **Tightrope** (sideline, near midfield)
- **Middle** (center of field at midfield)
- **Attack outlet** (just past midfield)

**Drill:**
1. Coach shoots on goal — goalie saves
2. Goalie yells "CLEAR!"
3. **Defenders pop out** to give goalie outlet options near goal
4. **Tightrope middie** sprints along the sideline — right on that line!
5. **Middle middie** sprints to center field — spread out from tightrope
6. **Attack** comes back to midfield as deep outlet
7. Goalie → defender → tightrope or middle → attack. Long passes!
8. Reset, rotate positions

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Pop out!"** — Defenders give the goalie options
- **"Tightrope!"** — Stay on the sideline
- **"Middle!"** — Spread to center
- **"Find space!"** — Move where the goalie can see you
- **"Long pass!"** — Don't dink it around, get it out

**Progression:**
- First 5 min: Walk through, coach directing
- Next 5 min: Full speed, no coach prompting
- Final 5 min: Add 1-2 riders for pressure

---

#### Station B: RIDE — "Guide Em Out"

**Objective:** When the other team gets the ball, the riders pressure the clearer and GUIDE him toward the sideline — same concept as defensive guiding. Don't overcommit, don't blow past — funnel him into bad passes or turnovers.
**Live players:** 4-5 (1 clearer with ball, 1 outlet, 2-3 riders)

**Setup:** Half field. Clearer starts with ball near goal. Outlet player 15-20 yards away. 2-3 riders ready to pressure.

**Drill:**
- Clearer tries to advance the ball or pass to the outlet
- Riders pressure: get in front, slow him down, **GUIDE him toward the sideline**
- Don't lunge, don't chase — be patient, force him to the sideline
- If he's near the sideline, second rider closes the door (it's a trap!)
- 3rd rider reads the outlet pass

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Guide em out!"** — Push toward the sideline, not the middle
- **"Patient!"** — Don't lunge or overcommit
- **"Stay in front!"** — Match his speed
- **"No easy pickups!"** — Pressure ground balls
- **"Read the outlet!"** — 3rd rider jumps the lane

**Progression:**
- First 5 min: 1v1 — clearer vs 1 rider, just guide him out
- Next 5 min: 2v2 — add an outlet and a 2nd rider
- Final 5 min: Live ride — clearer tries to escape, riders pressure and trap

### 7:05-7:30 — Clear vs Ride Live (25 min)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. Clearing team vs riding team.
**Live players:** ~6-8 per group (goalie + 2 defense + 2 middies + 1 attack vs 2-3 riders)

**Each rep:**
1. Coach shoots on goal — goalie saves
2. Goalie yells "CLEAR!" → defenders pop out, middies run tightrope/middle, attack at midfield outlet
3. Riders immediately pressure — guide em out, no easy pickups
4. Clearing team wins if ball crosses midfield. Riders win if turnover or held in defensive end.

**Rotation:** Fast — clearer goes to riding, riding goes to sideline, next group on. Keep tempo high.

**Coaching cues:**
- Clearers: **"Pop out! Tightrope! Middle! Long pass!"**
- Riders: **"Guide em out! Patient! No easy pickups!"**
- Everyone: **"FAST! Next group, GO!"**

Goal vs rider point on the board. Make it competitive.

## Key Terms for Tonight

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Clear** | Get the ball out of our defensive end with long passes |
| **Pop out** | Defenders move out from goal to give goalie outlet options |
| **Tightrope** | Middie runs along the sideline during a clear |
| **Middle** | Middie runs to center field during a clear |
| **Ride** | Pressure the other team's clear, try to get the ball back |
| **Guide em out** | Push the clearer toward the sideline (same as defensive guiding) |
| **No easy pickups** | Pressure ground balls — don't let them get it free |
| **Patient** | Don't lunge, don't overcommit — stay in front |
