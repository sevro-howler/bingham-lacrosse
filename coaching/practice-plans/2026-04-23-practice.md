## Schedule

### 6:00-6:10 — Wall Ball (10 min)
- Right hand: 50 throws/catches
- Left hand: 50 throws/catches
- Quick stick: 25 each hand
- **"Soft hands! Quick release! Both hands!"**

### 6:10-6:25 — Ground Ball Drills (15 min)

**Lone Wolf to Outlet (15 min):**
- 2 defenders + 1 ball carrier ("lone wolf"), all start on their bellies
- Ball on the ground in front of them
- Whistle → everyone scrambles up, 2v1 ground ball battle
- Lone wolf fights for the ball against 2 defenders
- Once lone wolf gets it, immediately throw to an outlet player
- **"Get up! Fight for it! Scoop it! Find the outlet! THROW!"**
- Rotate through so everyone gets reps as the lone wolf
- **Progression:**
  - First 5 min: walk through — get the concept (belly, up, scoop, outlet)
  - Next 5 min: full speed — competitive 2v1
  - Final 5 min: add pressure on the outlet pass (defender trails)

### 6:25-6:40 — Weaving Passes (15 min)

**3-man weave — full field (10 min):**
- 3 lines at the end line
- Middle player starts with ball, passes to one side, runs behind the receiver
- Receiver passes to the other side, runs behind
- Weave all the way down field, finish with a shot on goal
- **"Pass and go behind! Don't stop your feet! Lead your man!"**
- Progression: walk through → jog → 3/4 speed → full speed

**4-man weave (5 min):**
- Same concept, 4 lines
- More passes, more movement, faster reads
- **"Head up! Find the open man!"**

### 6:40-7:00 — 3v2 Drill (20 min)

**Objective:** 3 offense vs 2 defense around the goal. Offense must move the ball and get open. Defense plays Ball-Help.
**Live players:** 5 (3 offense + 2 defense)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. 3 offense (pinnies), 2 defense.

**Drill:**
- **First 5 min — 3v2, offense must complete 2 passes before shooting:**
  - Offense spreads out, moves the ball
  - Defense calls "Ball!" and "Help!" — switches on every pass
  - **"1 defender per offensive player!"**
  - Coach stops to check positioning

- **Next 5 min — 3v2 with called plays:**
  - Coach calls PINEAPPLE, PEANUT BUTTER, or [Name] is LION
  - Offense runs the set against live defense
  - Must complete 2 passes before shooting

- **Final 10 min — 3v2 continuous:**
  - Full speed, live
  - Goal or turnover → next 5 in
  - Offense that scores stays on, defense rotates
  - Keep score — builds competition

**Coaching cues:**
- Offense: **"Get open! Pass and move! Simple pass!"**
- Defense: **"Ball! Help! Guide him out! Patient!"**

### 7:00-7:30 — Full Scrimmage (30 min)

**7v7 full field with goalies. Game-like.**

- Faceoffs to start each possession
- Full clears, full transition, full offense/defense
- Play to goals — first to 3, then reset teams

**Coach watches for everything we've been teaching:**
- Offense: Run the plays, pass and move, get open, no hero ball
- Defense: Guide him out, Ball-Help, patient positioning
- Transition: Wheels! Clear with tightrope/middle
- Ground balls: Fight for it, outlet pass

**Let them play.** Call out good plays, not just mistakes.

## Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Scoop through** | Low hips, run through the ball, don't stop |
| **Hockey it** | Kick/push it out of traffic, then pick up |
| **Pass and go behind** | Weave: pass, then run behind the receiver |
| **Ball / Help** | Defense: 1 on ball, 1 reading and ready to slide |
| **3 passes** | Must complete before shooting in 4v4 |
| **Pass and move** | Catch, pass, relocate — feet never stop |
