#### Station A: DEFENSE — "Guide Him Out" (1v1 Positioning)

**Objective:** 1v1 defensive positioning. Get in front of the attacker and push him AWAY from goal — toward the sideline, not the middle. Don't blow past him, don't lunge — guide him.
**Live players:** 2 at a time (1v1)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. 2 lines on opposite sides of the field, each about 15 yards out from goal.
- **Line A (left side):** Attacker with ball
- **Line B (left side):** Defender

Same setup mirrored on the right side so both lines run simultaneously.

**Drill (15 min per group):**
- Attacker carries ball toward goal
- Defender gets in front — body between attacker and goal
- Defender's job: **push him away from goal, toward the sideline**
- NOT toward the middle of the field — always push OUT
- Don't blow past him, don't lunge — slide your feet, stay in front, GUIDE him
- After the rep, attacker goes to defender line, defender goes to attacker line on the OTHER side
- This way everyone plays both sides of the field

**Coaching points:**
- **"Get in his way!"** — Don't reach, don't chase. Get your body in front.
- **"Push him out!"** — Always toward the sideline, never toward the middle
- **"Feet first!"** — Slide your feet, stay low, stay balanced
- **"Don't blow past him!"** — Stay under control. You're guiding, not chasing.
- **"Which way are you pushing?"** — Quiz them. Answer: "AWAY from goal! Toward the sideline!"

**Progression:**
- **First 5 min:** Half speed — attacker jogs, defender works on positioning
- **Next 5 min:** 3/4 speed — attacker tries harder, defender must stay in front
- **Final 5 min:** Full speed — live 1v1, attacker tries to score, defender guides him out

**Key words:** "Guide him out!", "Push him to the sideline!", "Don't blow past!", "Feet first!"

#### Station B: OFFENSE — "Pineapple, Peanut Butter, Lion"

**Objective:** Run all 3 offensive sets on coach's call. Introduce Lion (pick and roll).
**Live players:** 4-5 (offense only first, add defense last 5 min)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. Cones marking positions for each set.

**Drill (15 min per group):**
- **First 3 min — Pineapple review:**
  - Diamond positions. X carries, open wing cuts, pass, shoot.
  - 3-4 reps. "Call it out: PINEAPPLE!"

- **Next 3 min — Peanut Butter review:**
  - Wide spread. Ball carrier picks a side, 1v1. Everyone else stays home.
  - 3-4 reps. "Call it out: PEANUT BUTTER!"

- **Next 5 min — Lion (NEW):**
  - Coach calls: "[Name] is LION!"
  - Lion player jogs to the top (left or right) and sets a pick
  - Ball carrier runs off the pick
  - Lion rolls off toward the goal — ball carrier passes to Lion
  - Lion catches and shoots
  - Walk through slowly, then speed up
  - Rotate who is Lion — everyone gets 2-3 reps setting the pick

- **Final 4 min — Random call:**
  - Coach calls "PINEAPPLE!", "PEANUT BUTTER!", or "[Name] is LION!"
  - Players set up the right formation and run it
  - Add 2 defenders for live reps if time
  - Focus: do they know where to go on each call?

**Key words:** "Pineapple!", "Peanut Butter!", "[Name] is Lion!"

### 7:00-7:30 — 4v4 Live (30 min)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. 4v4.
**Live players:** 8 at a time. Rotate full set of 4 on goal or turnover.

**How it works:**
1. Offense starts with ball at the top
2. Coach calls the set: "PINEAPPLE!", "PEANUT BUTTER!", or "[Name] is LION!"
3. Offense runs the set against live defense
4. Defense guides attacker out, can call "TRAP!" when he's near sideline
5. Play until **goal or turnover** → both teams come off, next 8 go on

**Rotation:** Full 4 on, full 4 off. Offense and defense swap each time they come back on.

**Focus:**
- **Offense: "SLOW DOWN!"** — Don't rush. Set up the play. Call the set. Run it.
- **Defense: "GUIDE HIM OUT!"** — Get in front, push to the sideline, set up the trap.

**Coach calls out:**
- Offense rushing: "SLOW DOWN! What's the play?"
- Defense chasing: "GUIDE HIM OUT! Don't blow past!"

Keep it moving — goal or turnover, new 8, go.
