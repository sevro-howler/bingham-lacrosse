#### Station A: "Get Open" — 3v2 Keep-Away

**Objective:** Move without the ball. Get open. Make short passes. Ball must move — no holding it.
**Live players:** 5 (3 offense + 2 defense)

**Setup:** 20x20 yard grid with cones. 3 offense (pinnies), 2 defense. 1 ball.

**Rules:**
- Offense tries to complete 7 passes in a row
- **You CANNOT pass back to the person who just passed to you**
- **You can only hold the ball for 3 seconds** — pass or lose it
- After you pass, you MUST move to a new spot
- Defense tries to intercept or force a bad pass

**Coaching points:**
- **"Move after you pass!"** — Don't pass and stand there
- **"Get where he can see you!"** — Don't hide behind a defender
- **"Short pass!"** — No cross-field bombs in a 20-yard grid
- **"Everyone touches!"** — Can't pass back to the same person
- Stop the drill if someone holds the ball: "3 SECONDS! Move the ball!"
- Stop the drill if someone stands still: "GET OPEN! Move your feet!"

**Progression:**
- **First 5 min:** 3v1 — easy, learn the movement
- **Next 5 min:** 3v2 — harder, have to work to get open
- **Final 5 min:** 3v3 — game-like pressure, must move to survive

**Key words:** "Get open!", "Move after you pass!", "Short pass!", "3 seconds!"

---

#### Station B: DEFENSE — "Guide Him Out" Review

**Objective:** Review 1v1 defensive positioning from Thursday. Get in front, push to sideline, don't blow past.
**Live players:** 2 at a time (1v1)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. 2 lines on opposite sides, ~15 yards from goal. Attacker + defender lines. Mirrored on both sides.

**Drill (15 min per group):**
- Attacker carries ball toward goal
- Defender gets in front — body between attacker and goal
- Push him AWAY from goal, toward the sideline — NOT the middle
- Don't blow past, don't lunge — slide feet, stay in front, GUIDE him
- Rotate: attacker → defender on the OTHER side

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Get in his way!"**
- **"Push him out!"**
- **"Feet first!"**
- **"Don't blow past!"**

**Progression:**
- First 5 min: Half speed
- Next 5 min: 3/4 speed
- Final 5 min: Full speed — live 1v1, attacker tries to score

### 7:00-7:30 — 4v4 Live (30 min)

**4v4 half field. Rotate full set of 4 on goal or turnover.**

**Setup:** Half field with goal. 4v4. Next 8 on sideline.

**TONIGHT'S RULES (non-negotiable):**
1. **Must complete 3 passes before you can shoot** — no hero ball
2. **Can't pass back to the person who passed to you** — ball must move around
3. **Coach calls the play** — PINEAPPLE, PEANUT BUTTER, or LION. Run it or lose possession.
4. If you go 1v3+ without passing: whistle, turnover, next group

**Each rep:**
1. Offense starts with ball at the top
2. Coach calls the set
3. Offense must complete 3 passes, running the called play
4. Defense plays Guide Him Out positioning
5. Goal or turnover → both teams off, next 8 on

**Focus:**
- **Offense: "GET OPEN! SIMPLE PASS! RUN THE PLAY!"**
- **Defense: "GUIDE HIM OUT!"**

**Coach calls out:**
- Someone holding the ball: "MOVE THE BALL! 3 seconds!"
- Someone standing still off-ball: "GET OPEN! Move your feet!"
- Hero ball: *whistle* "That's a turnover. We pass on this team."
- Good passing sequence: "THAT'S IT! That's how we play!"
- Defense chasing: "GUIDE HIM OUT!"

Keep it moving — goal or turnover, new 8, go.
