#### Station A: Princeton Weave (15 min)

**Setup:** Half field. 4 players in a box around the goal (2 up top, 2 on the wings). Ball starts at the top.

**Drill:**
- Pass to the wing, cut through behind the goal to the other side
- Receiver catches, passes to the next spot, cuts through
- Ball and players weave around the goal in a continuous pattern
- Everyone is always moving — pass and cut, pass and cut
- After 5 min of pattern work, add a shot: coach calls "SHOOT!" and whoever has the ball turns and fires

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Pass and cut!"** — Don't stand after you pass
- **"Keep the pattern!"** — Trust the rotation
- **"Head up!"** — Know where the next pass goes
- **"Fast feet, soft hands!"**

**Progression:**
- First 5 min: Walk through the pattern, get the rotation
- Next 5 min: Game speed — keep the weave clean and fast
- Final 5 min: Add "SHOOT!" calls — read and react out of the weave

---

#### Station B: Shooting Around Defender (15 min)

**Setup:** Blow-up defenders (or cones/coaches standing still) set up between the shooting line and the goal. 1-2 shooting lines.

**Objective:** Practice coming around the defender the RIGHT direction — protect your stick, hide it from the defender, then shoot.

**Drill:**
- Player carries ball toward the blow-up defender
- Come around the CORRECT side — stick away from the defender (hide your stick)
- If you're righty coming from the left: go around the defender's right so your stick is on the far side
- If you're righty coming from the right: switch hands or go around so stick is protected
- Get around the defender, shoot from the line

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Hide your stick!"** — Keep it away from the defender as you come around
- **"Wrong side!"** — If they go around exposing their stick, stop and reset
- **"Protect and shoot!"** — Come around clean, then fire
- **"Shooting line!"** — Don't run all the way to the crease

**Progression:**
- First 5 min: Walk through — which side do I go? Where's my stick?
- Next 5 min: Full speed — come around, shoot
- Final 5 min: Add a second defender to dodge around, then shoot

### 7:05-7:30 — 3v2 Rotate In (25 min)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. All together (or split in half if needed for pace).

**How it works:**
- 3 offense vs 2 defense, live
- Play until goal or turnover
- After the rep: offense goes to defense line, defense goes to sideline, next 3 offense step on
- Keep it fast — as soon as the rep ends, next group GO
- No standing around, always be ready to step on

**Coaching cues:**
- Offense: **"Pass and move! Get open! Run the play!"**
- Defense: **"Ball! Help! Guide him out!"**
- Everyone: **"FAST! Next group, GO!"**

Keep it high tempo the entire 25 minutes. Lots of reps.
