## Schedule

### 6:00-6:15 — Deliberate Wall Ball (15 min)

**Quality over quantity. Every rep has a target.**

- **5 min — Form throws:** Slow, focused throws. Target a specific spot on the wall every time. 50 reps right, 50 left.
- **5 min — Quick stick:** Catch and release with NO cradle. Both hands. Pick a spot, hit it.
- **5 min — Off-hand only:** Weak hand reps. Slow, deliberate. Build the muscle.

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Pick your spot!"** — Aim every throw
- **"Soft hands!"** — Catch like it's an egg
- **"Same form every time!"** — Repeatable mechanics

### 6:15-6:30 — 3v3 Keep-Away (Long Pass) (15 min)

**Setup:** Big rectangle (~25x35 yards) with a smaller square in the MIDDLE (~10x10) marked with cones. The middle square is OFF LIMITS — no one can enter it. Forces passes around or over the square = long passes.

**Rules:**
- 3 offense vs 3 defense in the outer area
- Complete as many passes in a row as you can
- **No one can enter the middle square** — must pass around it
- Defense tries to intercept or force a bad pass
- Hold ball more than 3 seconds = turnover

**Why this works:**
- Forces long, accurate passes (the kind that fail in games)
- Receivers have to move to find angles around the dead zone
- No short pass crutch — must commit to a real throw

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Long pass!"** — Lead them with a real throw
- **"Find the angle!"** — Move to get the open lane
- **"Soft and accurate!"** — Distance doesn't mean rocket arm

**Progression:**
- First 5 min: Just complete passes — no defense pressure
- Next 5 min: 3v3 live
- Final 5 min: Shrink the outer space, make it harder

### 6:30-6:45 — Princeton Weave (15 min)

**Setup:** 4 players in a box around the goal (2 up top, 2 on wings). Ball starts at top.

**Drill:**
- Pass to the wing, cut through behind the goal to the other side
- Receiver catches, passes to the next spot, cuts through
- Continuous weave around the goal
- After 5 min of pattern: coach calls "SHOOT!" — whoever has the ball fires

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Pass and cut!"** — Don't stand after you pass
- **"Keep the pattern!"** — Trust the rotation
- **"Fast feet, soft hands!"**

**Progression:**
- First 5 min: Walk the pattern
- Next 5 min: Game speed
- Final 5 min: Add SHOOT calls

---

### 6:45-7:00 — Plyometrics Challenges (15 min)

**Goal-based challenges. Coach sets a target: hit X reps in Y seconds with NO heels touching. Everyone goes at once. Coach watches for heels — count up "who got it cleanly?" at the end.**

---

**Drill 1 — Jackhammers (3 min):**
- **Challenge: 20 jackhammers in 10 seconds, no heels touching**
- Everyone goes on whistle
- Coach watches — any heel touch = OUT
- "Who got 20 clean in 10 seconds?" — hands up
- Run it 2-3 times, keep tightening the target

---

**Drill 2 — Pogo Jumps (3 min):**
- **2-foot pogos: 30 jumps in 20 seconds, no heels touching**
- Coach counts down: "20, 15, 10, 5..."
- Heel touches the ground = you stop. Count was your number.
- "Who got 30? Who got 25?"
- Round 2: **1-foot pogos: 20 in 20 seconds (10 each leg)**

---

**Drill 3 — Hurdle Jumps (3 min):**
- 5 small hurdles/cones in a line
- **Challenge: 5 hurdles in 5 seconds, stick every landing on your toes**
- "Stick" = no heel touch, no stumble, no extra step
- Everyone goes one at a time, coach times and watches
- "Who got 5 clean hurdles in 5 seconds?"

---

**Drill 4 — Triple Bounce Swing (3 min):**
- 3 quick bounces on the toes, then explode forward (arm swing, knee drive)
- **Challenge: 10 triple bounce swings in 30 seconds, no heels touching**
- "Bounce-bounce-bounce-GO! Bounce-bounce-bounce-GO!"
- Heel touch during bounces = OUT for that rep
- "Who got all 10 clean?"

---

**Drill 5 — A-Skips (3 min):**
- A-skip across the field (high knee, opposite arm, on toes)
- **Challenge: 20 yards of A-skips in 8 seconds, no heels touching**
- Everyone goes together, coach watches the line
- Heel touch = you didn't make it
- "Who got across in 8 seconds clean?"
- Run it back the other way to make 2 attempts

---

**Coach's job:**
- Set the target. "X reps in Y seconds, no heels touching."
- Watch heels. Call out kids who break form.
- Count clean finishers at the end. "5 of you got it!"
- Tighten the target each round to push them.

**Coaching cues:**
- **"ON YOUR TOES!"**
- **"Heel touched! Out!"**
- **"Faster! But CLEAN!"**

---

### 7:00-7:30 — Speedball Scrimmage with Tennis Balls (30 min)

**Lighter ball, faster game, no pads = pure stick skills and movement.**

**Setup:** Half field with goals (or cones as goals). Tennis balls only. 4v4 or 5v5.

**Rules:**
- Tennis balls only (smaller, lighter, harder to cradle)
- **No checks** — focus on positioning and movement
- 3-second hold limit (forces fast decisions)
- Must complete 2 passes before shooting
- Goal or turnover → rotate fresh players in

**Why this works:**
- Tennis balls are unforgiving — bad cradles get dropped
- Faster pace because the ball moves easier
- No-pad/no-check = can't rely on hits, must play position
- Builds soft hands and clean passes

**Coaching cues:**
- **"Soft hands!"** — Tennis balls bounce out of bad cradles
- **"Quick decisions!"** — 3 seconds, pass or lose it
- **"Move your feet!"**
- **"Position, not hits!"**

Keep it fun. Fast rotations. Score should rack up — celebrate good plays.
