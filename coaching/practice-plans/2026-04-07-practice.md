## Schedule

### 6:00-6:10 — "Get 20" Warmup (10 min)
- Partners, 10 yards apart
- Complete 20 clean passes/catches in a row
- If you drop it, start over
- Right hand first, then left hand
- **"Soft pass! Give a target!"**

### 6:10-6:25 — X Passing Drill (15 min)
- 4 lines in an X shape (corners of a box, ~15-20 yards apart)
- Player passes across to opposite line, then follows their pass and goes to the back of that line
- Ball moves in an X pattern — 2 balls going at once to keep it moving
- Start with right hand, switch to left hand halfway
- **Progression:**
  1. First 5 min: 1 ball, walk through the pattern
  2. Next 5 min: 2 balls, pick up the pace
  3. Final 5 min: 2 balls, full speed — keep your head up!
- **Coaching points:** Look where you're throwing, lead the receiver, catch and release quick

### 6:25-6:35 — Ground Ball Pickups (10 min)
- Lines at end line, coach rolls balls out
- Scoop through, don't rake — protect after pickup
- **"Hockey it if it's in traffic! No raking!"**
- **Progression:**
  1. First 5 min: solo pickups — focus on form (low hips, scoop through)
  2. Next 5 min: 1v1 ground ball races — competitive, full speed

#### Drill A: Clear — "Tightrope & Middle"

**Objective:** Review the clear. Goalie saves, defenders pop out to outlets, one middie runs the tightrope (along the sideline), one middie runs to the middle of the field. Long passes to move the ball out.
**Live players:** 6 (goalie + 2 defense + 2 middies + 1 attack at midfield)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. Cones marking:
- Tightrope (sideline, near midfield)
- Middle (center of field at midfield)
- Attack outlet (just past midfield)

**Drill (15 min per group):**
1. Coach shoots on goal — goalie saves
2. Goalie yells "CLEAR!"
3. **Defenders pop out** to give the goalie outlet options near goal
4. **Tightrope middie** sprints along the sideline — right on that line!
5. **Middle middie** sprints to center of field — spread out from tightrope
6. **Attack** comes back to midfield as deep outlet
7. Goalie → defender → tightrope or middle → attack. Long passes, get it out!
8. Reset, rotate positions so everyone learns each spot
- **First 5 min:** Walk through, coach directing: "Where's the tightrope? Where's the middle?"
- **Next 5 min:** Full speed, no coach prompting
- **Final 5 min:** Add 1-2 riders for pressure

**Key words:** "CLEAR!", "Tightrope!", "Middle!", "Pop out!", "Get to your spot!"

---

#### Drill B: Defense — "Ball-Help 3v2"

**Objective:** Review Ball-Help defense. 1 defender per offensive player — no doubling up! Switch on every pass.
**Live players:** 5 (3 offense + 2 defense)

**Setup:** Half field with goal. 3 offense (pinnies) spread around goal. 2 defense.

**Drill (15 min per group):**
1. Offense passes ball around the goal
2. On every pass: Help → Ball, Ball → Help
3. Defenders call it: "BALL!" "HELP!" on every switch
4. **"1 defender per offensive player!"**
- **First 5 min:** Slow passing, coach stops and checks positioning
- **Next 5 min:** Speed up passing, defenders must switch faster
- **Final 5 min:** Live — offense can score, defense plays Ball-Help
- If ball carrier is near sideline, coach can call "TRAP!" — practice the V funnel

**Key words:** "Ball!", "Help!", "Switch!", "1 per man!"
