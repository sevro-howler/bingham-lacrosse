# Fall 2026 Season Practice Plan — Bingham 3/4

**Team:** 13 players. Head coach Keaton Godfrey, assistant Joshua Howland.
**Practices:** Mondays and Wednesdays, 6:00-7:30 PM at SoJo Middle.
**Field:** east half of the 3/4 band 6:00-6:30 (K/1 has the west half), full 60x35 from 6:30.
**Games:** Saturdays, Sep 19 through Oct 24 (5 games, 7v7).

## The idea

Small team, big advantage: 13 kids means every boy gets double the reps of a 25-kid roster, and every boy plays big minutes on Saturdays. So fall = the fundamentals season. Two things and only two things:

1. **Pass and catch.** Under pressure, on the run, with either hand ready. If we complete more passes than the other team, we win 3/4 lacrosse. Period.
2. **Positioning.** Where do I stand? Where do I go when the ball moves? A kid who knows WHERE to be gets the ball more, and a team with spacing makes every pass 10 yards easier.

No new dodges, no fancy plays. IWY on everything (I do, We do, You do). Same terminology as spring: Man-Ball, Wheels, Shooting Line, Peanut Butter.

## Measurables (test every 2 weeks, track on a clipboard)

- **Partner pass test:** completed catches at 10 yards in 3 MINUTES, pairs (cones mark the distance). Total catches, drops just pause the count. All pairs test at once, boys count out loud, coaches spot-check. Record each pair's total.
- **On-the-run test:** completions out of 10 while jogging a 20-yard arc.
- **Spacing check (coach eye test):** during scrimmage, freeze play randomly; count how many kids are within 5 yards of another teammate. Goal: zero by October.

Boys should see their own numbers go up. That IS the season.

## Standing 90-minute skeleton

| Time | Block | Field | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6:00-6:10 | Arrival passing | East half | Pads ON, passing at 6:00 sharp (the standard is set). Partner passing progressions, both hands |
| 6:10-6:45 | Skill stations x3 | East half | 3 stations, 7 min each (35 min block). Groups of 4-4-5 |
| 6:45-7:05 | Concept install (IWY) | Full field | The week's positioning concept. I do, We do walk-through, You do at pace |
| 7:05-7:17 | Constraint scrimmage | Full field | 5v5 with a rule that forces the week's skill (see constraints) |
| 7:17-7:27 | Wheels finisher | Full field | Transition reps, everybody runs, ends with shots. Send them home sweaty and smiling |
| 7:27-7:30 | Huddle | — | One thing we learned, one thing for Saturday, cheer |

**Constraint scrimmage rules (rotate weekly):**
- *3-Pass Rule:* 3 completed passes before any shot
- *Everybody Touches:* all field players touch the ball before a shot counts (double points)
- *No Running with the Ball:* 3 steps max, forces heads-up passing (use sparingly, it is hard)
- *Silent Defense:* defense cannot talk, offense must call names on every pass
- *Freeze:* coach yells FREEZE, everyone plants. Spacing checked out loud, then play on

## Season arc

### Block 1: Hands (Aug 24 - Sep 2, 4 practices)
Every station is pass/catch. Stations: Deliberate Partner Passing (no wall at SoJo, so pairs replace wall ball), Soft Pass Keep-Away, Hockey for Ground Balls (ground ball INTO an outlet pass). If we get a bounce-back rebounder, it swaps in as a station; boys do real wall ball at home (send the Wall Ball Routine video to parents). Concept installs: ready hands + "ask for the ball" (stick up, call the name), catching on the move. Constraint: 3-Pass Rule all block. Baseline the measurables Wed Aug 26.

### Block 2: Where do I stand? (Sep 7* - Sep 16, 3-4 practices) — *no practice Labor Day Sep 7
Positioning install, one layer per practice:
1. **The shape:** 7v7 spacing. Nobody inside 10 yards of the ball carrier unless cutting. Teach with Freeze constraint.
2. **Offense shape: Peanut Butter** (the 2-2 from spring, simplified). Two high, two low. When ball moves, the shape breathes: nearest man supports, opposite man stretches.
3. **Defense shape: Man-Ball + Body Between.** Your man, your ball responsibilities. Wing Help on ground balls.
4. **Game prep (Wed Sep 16):** positions assigned for Saturday, Man-Ball Faceoff, Clear It Fast basics, full-field Wheels to Goal.

### Block 3: In-season cycle (Sep 21 - Oct 21, ~10 practices)
Weekly rhythm:
- **Monday = Fix + Hands.** First 30 min always pass/catch stations (never stops all fall). Concept block fixes the biggest thing from Saturday's game (watch for: bunching, naked ground balls, silent receivers).
- **Wednesday = Shape + Prep.** One positioning layer deepened + constraint scrimmage + Wheels finisher. Name Saturday's focus in the huddle.

Layer schedule (adjust to what games expose):
- Week of Sep 21: Clears. Breakout Pattern, Find Space to Clear. Goalie/defense outlet = a PASS, not a run
- Week of Sep 28: Transition. Wheels to Goal with numbers (2v1, 3v2). Look Up Pass
- Week of Oct 5: Off-ball offense. Cutting into space from Peanut Butter, Chase After Shot (possession is gold with a small roster)
- Week of Oct 12: Defense positioning. Ball-Help Sliding, keeping shape when tired (small team = tired legs, position beats speed)
- Week of Oct 19: Celebrate + play. Retest measurables, boys pick favorite drills, Everybody Touches scrimmage. Last game Oct 24

## Small-roster game management (for Saturdays)

- 7v7 with 13 = 6 subs. Rotate on a clock, not on goals, so every boy knows minutes are safe
- Position pairs: each boy learns ONE offensive spot and ONE defensive job this fall, not every position
- The measurables clipboard comes to games: count completed passes as a team stat, report it to the boys after ("we completed 31 passes today, our record"). They will chase that number

## Coaching cues (same voice, all fall)

- "Pick your spot!" (every throw aims)
- "Soft hands!" (catching)
- "Ask for it!" (stick up, call the name)
- "Spread the field!" / "FREEZE" (spacing)
- "Man-Ball!" (ground ball roles)
- "Ground ball INTO a pass!" (never scoop and stand)
- "Where's your triangle?" (support angles around the carrier)
