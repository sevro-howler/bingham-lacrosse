## Schedule

### 6:00-6:10 — Arrival Passing (10 min)

Boys are READY at 6:00: pads on, partner, passing. (The standard went out in GroupMe. Praise the boys who are throwing at 6:00 sharp, loudly.)

- Pairs, 10 yards apart, both hands
- Coaches walk the line correcting: elbow up, step to target, soft hands

**Cues:** "Pick your spot!" · "Soft hands!"

### 6:10-6:16 — BASELINE: Partner Pass Test (6 min, all pairs at once)

- Fixed pairs (keep them all season), cones at 10 yards for every pair, spread across the field
- **3 MINUTES on one whistle: total completed catches.** Drops do not reset, the count just pauses while the ball is down
- Boys count out loud, coaches and dads roam and spot-check, Joshua records every pair on the clipboard
- The 13th boy makes one trio: run it as a triangle and mark it on the sheet

### 6:16-6:50 — Stations x3 (7 min each)

13 boys → three groups (4-4-5). One coach per station.

**Station 1 — Deliberate Partner Passing**
- 3 min form throws: partner holds stick head as target, thrower CALLS the spot before every throw ("right ear!"), 10 and switch
- 2 min quick-stick: 7 yards, no cradle, count consecutive completions out loud
- 2 min weak hand only: 5 yards, slow and deliberate

**Station 2 — Soft Pass Keep-Away** (from drill library)
- 3v1 (or 3v2 with 5) in a 15x15 box
- Offense: move to open space, ask for it (stick up, call the name)
- 3 seconds max holding the ball
- **Common mistake:** hiding behind the defender. Cue: "Get to where he can SEE you!"

**Station 3 — Hockey for Ground Balls** (from drill library)
- Ground ball INTO an outlet pass, never a naked scoop
- Coach rolls ball out, two boys battle, winner scoops and MUST hit the outlet man immediately
- Outlet man's job: sprint to open space, stick up, call the name
- **Cues:** "Man-Ball!" · "Ground ball INTO a pass!"

### 6:50-7:10 — Wheels, with Catching on the Run built in (20 min)

- Wheels to Goal, 2v1 waves: runner carries down the wing and PASSES to his partner. Defender slides to the ball. Ball goes right back to the runner (still running!) for the shot. 2 passes, then shoot
- The lesson the defender teaches: when he slides to you, your partner is open. Pass and it is a layup
- The receiving boy catches ON THE MOVE every wave: lead him with the pass, never let him stop his feet. Show the wrong way once (flat-footed catch, defender catches up), then run it
- **On-the-Run measurable:** tally each boy's catches out of his first 10 wave receptions on the clipboard. No separate drill, the waves are the test
- Every wave ends with a shot; Chase After Shot applies: nearest boy sprints the rebound
- Run it hot, coaches loud. **Cues:** "Run through the catch!" · "Lead him!"

### 7:10-7:30 — Constraint Scrimmage: 3-Pass Rule (20 min)

- 5v5 (subs rotate every 3 min on the whistle, everyone plays)
- **3 completed passes before ANY shot.** Coach counts out loud, parents can too
- Goal after 3+ passes = 1 point. Goal after 5+ passes = 2 points (announce it midway, watch what happens)
- FREEZE once or twice: everyone plants, look around, "who is bunched?" Then play on
- End on time, quick "beat your number in two weeks" message as they break
