## 5. Game-day operations: hosting, field setup, score table

How it worked in spring: when Bingham hosts, Collin emails the hosting schedule early in the week (game slots every ~75 minutes on 2 fields). Host program handles field setup and score table for all games that day.

Recurring failure to avoid: Collin sent at least four "Bingham Missing Game Scores!" emails in spring. Scores must be reported after every game week.

Standing game-day checklist (Saturdays, Sep 19 - Oct 24):
- Friday: confirm hosting schedule, assign volunteers per field slot
- Field setup crew (arrive 60-75 min early): lines painted/checked, goals placed and anchored (hence the welding), cones, trash cans out
- Score table per field: table, chairs, scoreboard/flip score, game clock/horn, printed schedule, scoresheets
- Volunteer roles per hosted slot: 1 score table + 1 field marshal minimum; recruit at parents meeting, schedule via GroupMe weekly
- After games: goals stored/secured, trash swept, field walk
- Sunday/Monday: report all Bingham scores to IMLAX (owner: Joshua or delegate one coach per team; do not repeat spring's missing-scores nags)

What the last two seasons teach about hosting:
- IMLAX pays the host program for game-day staffing (Spring 2026: $206 credited for the Mar 21 hosting day). Bingham paid teen score-table workers $12/hr from it ($204 total that day) — so the score table can be PAID teen jobs, not begged-for parent volunteers. Recruit HS players / older siblings
- Host must have fields set 30 minutes early with impartial, engaged table monitors (IMLAX rule)
- Field striping is scheduled by IMLAX through KW Striping (Kris Whitmore, 801-699-2407) — but district mowing erased lines twice in Fall 2025; check lines Thursday and request touch-ups through Collin early
- Score reporting into Otto Sport was Bingham's #1 compliance failure BOTH seasons (4 nags in fall, 5 in spring, K/1 lagged all season). Fix: every team has a named score reporter; scores entered by Monday; Joshua spot-checks Tuesdays
- QA every schedule Collin sends: Bingham caught double-bookings and time discrepancies both seasons; assume the first pass has errors
