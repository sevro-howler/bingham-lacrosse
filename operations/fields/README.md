| Zone | Size | Who | When |

Key constraints:
- A regulation boys field (110x60) only fits N-S; 60 + 35 of width won't fit side by side (~78 usable), so the clock resolves sharing.
- GOAL STORAGE: school requires all nets stored on the NORTHEAST side of the field. Never in front of fences with signs (teacher evacuation spots).
- Trailer in the west parking lot. East fence line is the property boundary.
