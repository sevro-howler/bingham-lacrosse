Layout logic
  • The east field measures about 75-80 yards east-west and 130 yards north-south. A regulation boys field is 110 x 60, so a full field only fits running NORTH-SOUTH, and a regulation-width field (60) plus the 3/4 field (35) will not fit side by side (95 needed, ~78 available). The clock resolves it.
  • BIG FIELD (58 x 85, goals north and south): 5/6 owns it 5:00-6:00; from 6:00-6:30 the 30-minute overlap splits it at the dashed line, 7/8 warming up on the north half while 5/6 finishes on the south half; 6:30-8:00 it is all 7/8 at full length. Josh Jorgensen coaches both teams, so the handoff is his.
  • 3/4 gets a true full 3/4 game field (60 x 35, east-west) from 6:00-7:30, south of the big field. 2/3 gets a field-shaped 36 x 22 in the pocket northeast of the building, 5:30-6:45, running east-west like their game fields. K/1 (5:30-6:30) spans the west half of the 3/4 band as its own little field; 3/4 runs its first half hour (6:00-6:30) on the east half, then takes the full 60 x 35 when K/1 wraps at 6:30.
  • Peak overlap is 6:00-6:30 when 5/6, 2/3, and 3/4 are all on and 7/8 is arriving. This layout holds everyone at once.
  • GOAL STORAGE (school requirement, Aug 2026): all nets must be stored on the NORTHEAST side of the field, marked in blue. Never store nets in front of fences with signs on them; those are teacher evacuation spots. The equipment trailer lives in the west parking lot; the east fence line is the property boundary.
